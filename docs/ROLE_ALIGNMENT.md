## 2. In-Depth Engineering Competency Narratives

### Competency A: Untrusted LLM Boundary & Deterministic Domain Policy
*How PropRelay demonstrates this*: Rather than relying on fragile system prompts to enforce business rules, PropRelay treats the LLM strictly as an unauthenticated natural language parser. All state-mutating actions pass through `BookingPolicyService`, which validates entity existence, checks calendar collision, requires a short-lived `PendingAction` confirmation token, and enforces atomic transactions under mutex locks.

### Competency B: Real-Time Audio Transport & Acoustic Optimization
*How PropRelay demonstrates this*: In voice AI, pipeline latency is dominated by acoustic endpointing (EOU) and speech synthesis chunking. By replacing default 0.5s endpointing with a tuned 0.3s dynamic window and implementing sentence-boundary streaming in Kokoro TTS, PropRelay reduced end-of-utterance delay by 447ms and achieved first audible speech delivery in 408ms, cutting total turn latency by 28.2% on reference hardware.

### Competency C: Evaluation Rigor & Deterministic Trace Judges
*How PropRelay demonstrates this*: Most conversational AI evaluations rely on LLM-as-a-judge prompts that introduce non-deterministic grading and cost money. PropRelay features 8 deterministic Python judges inspecting typed execution traces. The suite evaluates 25 distinct multi-turn trajectories (including adversarial prompt injections, impersonation attempts, and speech corrections) with automated release gating and failure fixture dumps.
